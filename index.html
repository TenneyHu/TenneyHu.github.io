Tianyi (Tenney) Hu

I will be joining the CLAN Lab, Department of Computer Science at Aarhus University September 2025 as a PhD student in Natural Language Processing under the supervision of Akhil Arora. I received my Master’s degree from the University of Copenhagen, advised by Daniel Hershcovich and Maria Maistro. Before that, I was a Senior Researcher in Basic Search Team at Baidu Search during 2018-2022, where I worked on improving the timeliness of the search engine, I recieved my BSc in Software Engineering at Dalian University of Technology in 2019.

Research Interests

My PhD research will focus on leveraging LLMs to support human knowledge-seeking. I'm currently interested in Natural Language Processing and Information Retrieval, particularly in areas such as Human-Centered NLP, Computational Social Science, and cultural studies in NLP.
